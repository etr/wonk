2. [x] **security-reviewer** | `src/elide.rs:494` | input-validation
   *Addressed (majors sweep, 2026-10-02):* already fixed upstream (fail-soft guard + tests).
   render_window panics when the 1-based line window is inverted (start_line > end_line, including clamped cases such as start_line=usize::MAX with a small end_line): first_row resolves past last_row, so window_start > window_end and `out.push_str(&source[last..window_end])` slices with start > end ('byte range starts at 79 but ends at 60', confirmed by probe at src/elide.rs:494).
